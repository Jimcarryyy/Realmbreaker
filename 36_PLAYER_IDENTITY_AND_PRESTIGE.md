# 36_PLAYER_IDENTITY_AND_PRESTIGE.md

## Purpose

Defines every system that communicates a player's accomplishments, reputation, prestige, and identity within Realmbreaker.

Prestige systems exist to recognize dedication, mastery, exploration, skill, and community contributions rather than providing unfair combat advantages.

Players should feel proud when others recognize what they have achieved.
