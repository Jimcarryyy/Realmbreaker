# 📈 Content Expansion & Live Ops

## 🔄 The "Season of the Heavens" Cycle
To maintain high retention, Realmbreaker will operate on a 3-month seasonal cycle.

### Phase A: The Discovery (Month 1)
*   Introduction of a new **Secret Realm** (Dungeon).
*   New "Dao Path" (Elemental specialization) added to the game.

### Phase B: The Escalation (Month 2)
*   **World Events:** "Beast Tides" where players must defend the Mortal Plane cities.
*   Sect War tournaments for territory control.

### Phase C: The Transcendence (Month 3)
*   A new **Major Realm** is unlocked (e.g., moving from Nascent Soul to Soul Transformation).
*   Level cap increase and new high-tier crafting recipes.

## 🛠️ Sustainability Strategy
1.  **Community-Driven Lore:** The winning Sect of a season gets their name etched into the lore of the next map update.
2.  **Profession Expansions:** Regularly adding new "Blueprints" for Blacksmiths and "Formulas" for Alchemists to keep the economy moving.
3.  **Performance Optimization Patches:** Bi-weekly technical updates to ensure the game remains smooth as more features are added.

## 💰 Ethical Monetization
*   **The Path Pass:** Seasonal rewards (Cosmetic only: Weapon skins, Auras, Titles).
*   **Convenience:** Inventory expansions and "Qi-Gathering Lanterns" (Slightly faster meditation, but no stat boosts).
