# 📜 Lore & Narrative: The Breaking of the Dao

## 🏛️ The Great Cataclysm
Eons ago, the "Heavenly Dao" was a perfect, unbreakable law. But the first **Realmbreaker**—a cultivator of unknown origin—shattered the pillar of heaven to prevent a cosmic reset. This event, known as **The Great Shattering**, broke the world into the current layers and allowed mortals to steal "forbidden" power.

## ⚔️ The Current Era
You are a "Pathfinder" in a world where the old gods are dead, but their remnants (Ancient Beasts) still guard the fragments of the Dao.

### The Three Great Factions:
1.  **The Celestial Orthodoxy:** They seek to repair the Dao and impose strict laws on cultivation. They value order and tradition.
2.  **The Myriad Demon Sect:** They believe the Shattering was a gift of freedom. They value raw power, chaos, and individual strength.
3.  **The Rogue Path:** Players who refuse to join the major sects, carving their own path through the world.

## 🌌 The "Realmbreaker" Philosophy
To become a "Realmbreaker" is to do more than just grow stronger. It is to fundamentally **defy the laws of the world**. 
*   *Example:* If the world says humans cannot fly, a Realmbreaker crafts wings of Qi and takes to the sky. 
*   *Goal:* The ultimate end-game goal is to reach the "Heaven Shattering" realm and decide the fate of the next world cycle.
