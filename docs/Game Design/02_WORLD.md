# 🌍 World Design: The Shattered Heavens

## 🗺️ World Structure
Realmbreaker uses a **Layered Open World** approach. The world is divided into "Planes," each requiring a specific Cultivation Realm or specialized Artifact to traverse.

### 1. The Mortal Plane (The Foundation)
*   **Vibe:** Traditional Eastern landscapes—bamboo forests, serene rivers, and ancient merchant cities.
*   **Key Mechanic:** This is the social hub. Most player-to-player trading and early sect recruitment happen here.
*   **Landmarks:** 
    *   *The Azure Cloud City:* A neutral trade hub.
    *   *The Weeping Bamboo Forest:* Early-game resource zone.

### 2. The Spirit Peaks (Tier 2 - Foundation Establishment)
*   **Vibe:** Floating mountains, heavy mist, and vertical architecture.
*   **Key Mechanic:** **Verticality.** Players must unlock "Step-on-Air" or Flying Swords to navigate this zone effectively.
*   **Environmental Hazard:** *Qi Turbulence.* Players with low cultivation speed are pushed back by the wind.

### 3. The Void Tainted Lands (Tier 3 - Core Formation)
*   **Vibe:** Eerie, crystalline landscapes where the laws of physics are broken.
*   **Key Mechanic:** **Survival.** The atmosphere is toxic; players must consume "Purification Pills" (from Alchemists) to stay here.

## 💎 Spirit Veins & Resource Nodes
The world is dotted with **Spirit Veins**. 
*   These are high-yield resource zones for Blacksmiths and Alchemists.
*   They serve as the primary **Open-World PvP Hotspots**.
*   Sects can "Claim" a vein to generate passive resources for their members.

## 🚪 Secret Realms (Instanced Content)
Hidden portals that appear randomly or under specific conditions (e.g., during a thunderstorm).
*   **Logic:** These are mechanically heavy dungeons with puzzles and bosses.
*   **Rewards:** Legendary cultivation manuals and rare crafting catalysts.
