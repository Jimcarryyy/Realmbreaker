# 💎 Project Decisions

*   **Decision 01:** Use a centralized `SpendQi()` function to prevent logic fragmentation. (Applied in Phase 2).
*   **Decision 02:** Adopt a "Validation vs. Execution" model for skills to simplify adding new techniques.
*   **Decision 03:** Implement 12 Major Realms with 9 Minor Orders to provide a granular sense of progression.
*   **Decision 04:** Zone 1 (Mortal Plane) is the primary testing ground for the "Grounded" combat phase.
